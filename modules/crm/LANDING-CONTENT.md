# Landing Narrative — DUCK ECOSYSTEM

## Hero

**DUCK ECOSYSTEM**

**One creative machine. Many systems. One memory.**

Independent tools become one operating environment without losing their original identity.

CTA: `ENTER THE MACHINE`

## Section 01 — The problem

The studio has projects, stems, DAWs, plugins, ideas, AI systems, client portals and experiments. The problem is not the lack of tools. The problem is that the tools do not share a common language.

## Section 02 — The thesis

Duck Ecosystem does not replace the tools. It gives them a nervous system.

## Section 03 — The machine

`INTENT → PLAN → CAPABILITY → APPROVAL → ACTION → EVENT → MEMORY`

Animate each transition as a signal travelling through a dark instrument panel.

## Section 04 — The organs

**STUDIO** — production and project truth.

**ZION** — experience and presentation.

**GEMA** — creative instruments and identity.

**LOCAL** — workstation, DAW and plugins.

**OMEGA / AION** — intelligence, planning and agents.

**EVIDENCE** — audit, provenance and truth.

## Section 05 — Live truth

Every capability visibly declares:

`REAL` / `SIMULATED` / `PLANNED` / `UNKNOWN`

The interface must make uncertainty look intentional, not broken.

## Section 06 — Demonstration

Input: **Prepare Therapist for Duck.**

Show the request becoming a plan, discovering systems, asking for approval, routing work and producing an auditable result.

## Section 07 — Originality preserved

The original repositories remain intact. Duck Ecosystem is the layer between them.

## Section 08 — The future

The same contract can connect new AI models, local agents, DAWs, plugins, devices and services without redesigning the core.

## Final

**THE STUDIO IS NO LONGER A COLLECTION OF TOOLS.**

**IT IS A MACHINE.**
